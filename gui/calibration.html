Prediction calibration — is a confident call actually correct?

676 held-out predictions (29 sealed samples × drivers), predicted vs known labels.

1 · Re-deriving the call thresholds fixed the over-calling

With the old Youden thresholds (grey, dotted), call accuracy collapsed to ~27–37% in the 0.7–0.9 band — the model over-called and mid-high-probability calls were mostly wrong. Re-deriving each mutation's threshold to maximize F1 (teal) lifts that band to ~69–91% and overall held-out call accuracy from 0.73 → 0.86.

2 · What the model reports vs the truth — and what calibration changes

The red line is the confidence the model reports (its raw probability). The teal line is the actual chance the mutation is present. The shaded gap is the over-confidence: a call reported at 0.85 is truly present only ~26% of the time. Calibration replaces the red claim with the teal reality — that teal value is the "chance ✓" the board now shows. The bigger the gap, the more calibration changes.
